Tripwire practice pages

These pages copy the shape of common scams so you can see Tripwire step in. Every company, name, phone number and amount on them is invented.

No install needed. On the hosted copy of these pages, Tripwire runs right in your browser: the same checks and the same screens as the extension, through Firebase AI Logic. If your Chrome has its on-device model, the check runs on your device; otherwise personal details are removed first and the cloud model is asked. With the extension installed, the extension takes over (and adds download protection and family alerts).

  • A message from “your bank”

    Open it, then click into the card number box.

  • A “your computer is infected” warning

    Open it, then try the phone link or the download button.

  • A demand to pay with gift cards

    Open it and read it. Then click into the code box.

  • An investment that cannot lose

    Open it, then click into the wallet address box.

  • A polite email about a refund "mistake"

    A quieter scam with no threats. Open it, choose "Why?", then "See exactly what was sent" to read the redacted text for yourself.

  • An ordinary shop checkout

    Tripwire should stay silent here, even when you type a card number.
