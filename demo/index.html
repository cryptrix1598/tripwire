Tripwire practice pages

These pages copy the shape of common scams so you can see Tripwire step in. Every company, name, phone number and amount on them is invented, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

  • A message from “your bank”

    Open it, then click into the card number box.

  • A “your computer is infected” warning

    Open it, then try the phone link or the download button.

  • A demand to pay with gift cards

    Open it and read it. Then click into the code box.

  • An investment that cannot lose

    Open it, then click into the wallet address box.

  • An ordinary shop checkout

    Tripwire should stay silent here, even when you type a card number.
